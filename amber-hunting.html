Amber hunting with a map and forecast

RavRadar helps you choose a time and place for amber hunting on Danish coasts. The amber map brings coastal areas together with calculated weather and sea conditions. The amber forecast shows how conditions are expected to develop. This amber outlook is for beginners and experienced amber hunters alike.

What does the amber forecast tell you?

RavRadar assesses conditions, not the number of pieces of amber on a beach. You can choose between searching on the beach and in the water. Currents, waves, wind and water level are part of the overview. A high score never guarantees a find. Read the displayed warnings, especially while the weather history is still being built.

Amber hunting nets and amber torches

An amber hunting net, called a kese or ravkese in Danish, is used to collect material in shallow water and search it for amber. An amber torch or amber flashlight is usually a UV light that can make amber easier to spot in the dark. Equipment does not guarantee amber or make dangerous sea conditions safe. RavRadar does not sell equipment.

Learn to read the sea and coast
